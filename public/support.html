Help with Sajilo Nepali

सजिलो नेपाली: Nepali for children, on the web and on iPhone and iPad.

What is free, and what is the Family Pass?

In the iPhone and iPad app, Chapter 1 is free. The Sajilo Family Pass unlocks every chapter, story, rhyme and game for everyone on the device. It starts with a 7-day free trial, then costs $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year (prices may vary by country).

I already have the Family Pass. How do I get it on a new iPhone or iPad?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the app, tap any lesson with a 🔒, answer the grown-ups' question, then tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel?

On the iPhone or iPad: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions, then Sajilo Family Pass, then Cancel Subscription. In the app you can also open For grown-ups, then Family Pass: manage subscription. To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Every picture, sound and story is inside the app.

I can't hear anything.

Turn the volume up with the side buttons, and check that sound is on at the top of the home screen.

Where is my child's progress kept?

Only on your device. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes the progress. See the privacy policy.
