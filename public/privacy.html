Privacy policy

Sajilo Nepali (सजिलो नेपाली), the website sajilonepali.app and the iPhone and iPad app. Last updated 15 November 2026.

The short version

Sajilo Nepali does not collect any information about you or your child. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app never sends anything to us or to any other company.

What stays on your device

To remember where your child is, the app saves a few things only on your own device: the players' names and their pictures chosen in the app, stars, stickers, lessons finished, and settings such as sound on or off. Nothing leaves the device. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) deletes all of it.

Microphone

Some activities let your child record themselves saying a word and hear it played back, and grown-ups can record family words in their own voice. These recordings stay only on your device: they are never uploaded or sent anywhere. The app asks for permission before it uses the microphone, and you can turn it off at any time in the device's settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions in the iPhone and iPad app are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID, email or payment details. Apple's own privacy policy applies to purchases: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The app is made for children and their families. Because it collects no personal information at all, it does not collect any personal information from children. Anything that leaves the app, such as sharing or buying, is behind a grown-ups' check.

Other companies

None. The app contains no third-party code that collects data, and even its fonts are built in.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
