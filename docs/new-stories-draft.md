# Two new daytime stories: please check the Nepali

These are drafts. Nothing is recorded yet. Mark any line you want changed (spelling, word choice, how a child at home would say it), or reply **ok** and I will record them in your voice.

Both stories start with the usual **एकादेशमा...** and end with **भन्नेलाई फूलको माला...**, which are already recorded.

## चतुर खरायो र सिंह · The Clever Rabbit and the Lion

**1.** हरियो जङ्गलमा धेरै जनावरहरू खुसीसाथ बस्थे।  
_hariyo jangalmaa dherai janaawarharu khusisaath basthe._  
In a green forest, many animals lived happily.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **चिरबिर** (a bird sings: chirbir!)

**2.** तर त्यहाँ एउटा रिसाहा सिंह पनि थियो। ऊ गर्ज्यो, ह्वार्र!  
_tara tyahaan euta risaahaa singha pani thiyo. u garjyo, hwaarr!_  
But a grumpy lion lived there too. He roared: rawrr!  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **ह्वार्र** (the lion roars: hwaarr!)

**3.** सिंहले भन्यो, हरेक दिन एउटा जनावर मेरो गुफामा आउनुपर्छ!  
_singhale bhanyo, harek din euta janaawar mero guphaamaa aaunuparchha!_  
The lion said, “Every day, one animal must come to my cave!”  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **गुफा** (the lion's cave)

**4.** सबै जनावरहरू डरले थरथर काँपे।  
_sabai janaawarharu darale tharthar kaanpe._  
All the animals shook with fear, thar-thar.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **थरथर** (shaking: thar-thar!)

**5.** एक दिन सानो खरायोको पालो आयो।  
_ek din saano kharaayoko paalo aayo._  
One day, it was the little rabbit's turn.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **खरायो** (the little rabbit)

**6.** खरायो बिस्तारै बिस्तारै हिँड्यो, र एउटा उपाय सोच्यो।  
_kharaayo bistaarai bistaarai hindyo, ra euta upaaya sochyo._  
The rabbit walked slowly, slowly, and thought of a plan.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **टुक्क** (a bright idea: tukka!)

**7.** सिंह रिसले चिच्यायो, किन यति ढिलो आएको?  
_singha risale chichyaayo, kina yati dhilo aaeko?_  
The lion shouted angrily, “Why are you so late?”  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **ह्वार्र** (an angry roar!)

**8.** खरायोले भन्यो, बाटोमा अर्को सिंहले मलाई रोक्यो!  
_kharaayole bhanyo, baatomaa arko singhale malaai rokyo!_  
The rabbit said, “Another lion stopped me on the way!”  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **खरायो** (the clever rabbit)

**9.** सिंह झन् रिसायो। मलाई त्यो सिंह देखाऊ!  
_singha jhan risaayo. malaai tyo singha dekhaau!_  
The lion got even angrier. “Show me that lion!”  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **ह्वार्र** (rawrr!)

**10.** खरायोले सिंहलाई एउटा गहिरो इनारमा लग्यो।  
_kharaayole singhalaai euta gahiro inaarmaa lagyo._  
The rabbit led the lion to a deep well.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **इनार** (a deep well)

**11.** सिंहले पानीमा आफ्नै छायाँ देख्यो र गर्ज्यो। इनारबाट पनि आवाज आयो, ह्वार्र!  
_singhale paanimaa aaphnai chhaayaan dekhyo ra garjyo. inaarbaata pani aawaaj aayo, hwaarr!_  
The lion saw his own reflection and roared. The well roared back: rawrr!  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **छायाँ** (the lion's reflection)

**12.** सिंहले इनारमा हाम फाल्यो, छप्लक्क! भिजेको सिंह लाजले जङ्गल छोडेर भाग्यो।  
_singhale inaarmaa haam phaalyo, chhaplakka! bhijeko singha laajale jangal chhodera bhaagyo._  
The lion jumped into the well: splash! The soaking-wet lion ran away from the forest in shame.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **छप्लक्क** (splash!)

**13.** सबै जनावरले भने, स्याबास, चतुर खरायो!  
_sabai janaawarle bhane, syaabaas, chatur kharaayo!_  
All the animals cheered, “Well done, clever rabbit!”  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **स्याबास** (well done!)

**Question for big learners at the end:** खरायोले सिंहलाई कहाँ लग्यो?  
_kharaayole singhalaai kahaan lagyo?_ · Where did the rabbit take the lion?  
Right answer: **इनार** (a well). Other choices: रूख (a tree), घर (a house)

## पाण्डुको चङ्गा · Pandu's Kite

**1.** दशैँ आयो! आकाश निलो छ, हावा सरर चल्छ।  
_dashain aayo! aakaash nilo chha, haawaa sarara chalchha._  
Dashain is here! The sky is blue and the breeze blows, sarara.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **सरर** (the breeze: sarara!)

**2.** पाण्डुले आफ्नो रातो चङ्गा र लट्टाई लियो।  
_paandule aaphno raato changaa ra lattaai liyo._  
Pandu took his red kite and his string spool.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **लट्टाई** (the kite-string spool)

**3.** चङ्गा माथि, माथि, धेरै माथि उड्यो!  
_changaa maathi, maathi, dherai maathi udyo!_  
The kite flew up, up, very high!  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **चङ्गा** (the kite)

**4.** अचानक, हावाले चङ्गालाई डाँडापारि उडाएर लग्यो। ओहो!  
_achaanak, haawaale changaalaai daandaapaari udaaera lagyo. oho!_  
Suddenly, the wind blew the kite over the hill. Oh no!  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **ओहो** (oh no!)

**5.** चङ्गा खोज्न जाँदा पाण्डुले दुईवटा बाटो देख्यो। कुन बाटो जाने?  
_changaa khojna jaandaa paandule duiwataa baato dekhyo. kun baato jaane?_  
Looking for the kite, Pandu found two paths. Which way should he go?  
👉 Choice: **झोलुङ्गे पुल (the swing bridge)** or **नदीको बाटो (the river path)**

**6 (bridge path).** पाण्डु झोलुङ्गे पुलमा हिँड्यो। पुल हल्लियो, झुल झुल!  
_paandu jholunge pulmaa hindyo. pul halliyo, jhul jhul!_  
Pandu walked across the swing bridge. It swayed: jhul jhul!  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **झुल झुल** (the bridge sways: jhul jhul!)

**7 (bridge path).** पुलपारि एउटा बाँदरले रूखमा अड्केको चङ्गा देखायो।  
_pulpaari euta baandarle rukhmaa adkeko changaa dekhaayo._  
Across the bridge, a monkey pointed at the kite stuck in a tree.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **बाँदर** (a monkey)

**8 (bridge path).** बाँदर रूखमा चढ्यो, र चङ्गा झारिदियो।  
_baandar rukhmaa chadhyo, ra changaa jhaaridiyo._  
The monkey climbed the tree and brought the kite down.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **चङ्गा** (the kite!)

**9 (river path).** पाण्डु नदीको किनारै किनार हिँड्यो। पानी कलकल बग्यो।  
_paandu nadiko kinaarai kinaar hindyo. paani kalkal bagyo._  
Pandu walked along the riverbank. The water flowed, kalkal.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **कलकल** (the river: kalkal!)

**10 (river path).** ढुङ्गामाथि बसेको एउटा भ्यागुतोले चङ्गा देखायो।  
_dhungaamaathi baseko euta bhyaagutole changaa dekhaayo._  
A frog sitting on a rock pointed at the kite.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **भ्यागुतो** (a frog)

**11 (river path).** भ्यागुतो फुत्त उफ्र्यो, र चङ्गा ल्याइदियो।  
_bhyaaguto phutta uphryo, ra changaa lyaaidiyo._  
The frog hopped, phutta!, and brought the kite back.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **फुत्त** (hop: phutta!)

**12.** पाण्डुले भन्यो, धेरै धेरै धन्यवाद, साथी!  
_paandule bhanyo, dherai dherai dhanyabaad, saathi!_  
Pandu said, “Thank you so much, friend!”  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **धन्यवाद** (thank you!)

**13.** घाम अस्ताउँदै थियो। पाण्डुको चङ्गा फेरि आकाशमा नाच्यो।  
_ghaam astaaundai thiyo. paanduko changaa pheri aakaashmaa naachyo._  
The sun was setting. Pandu's kite danced in the sky again.  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **घाम** (the setting sun)

**14.** पाण्डु खुसी हुँदै घर फर्कियो। अर्को पटक, अर्को बाटो जाऔँ!  
_paandu khusi hundai ghar pharkiyo. arko patak, arko baato jaaun!_  
Pandu went home happy. Next time, let's try the other path!  
🔊 Tap spot on the picture: **घर** (home)

**Question for big learners at the end:** पाण्डुको चङ्गा कस्तो रङको थियो?  
_paanduko changaa kasto rangko thiyo?_ · What color was Pandu's kite?  
Right answer: **रातो** (red). Other choices: निलो (blue), पहेँलो (yellow)

## Notes

- The lion story is the old Panchatantra fable, softened for young children: the lion jumps into the well and runs away soaking wet, instead of drowning.
- In the kite story, the child picks the path on page 5, so each reading is 11 pages and both paths together are 14.
- Each page also has one thing to tap on the picture (🔊 above). I will place those once the pictures are in.
